BEFORE EACH SESSION

5–8 minute warm-up

  1. March in place — 60 seconds
  2. Shoulder circles — 10 each direction
  3. Hip hinges — 10 reps
  4. Slow chair squats — 8 reps
  5. Easy arm swings and ankle circles
HOW TO PROGRESS

Keep it gradual

Weeks 1–2: Learn the movements with 2 sets.

Weeks 3–4: If form and recovery are good, add a set or 1–2 reps.

Weeks 5–8: Up to 3 sets for main exercises if manageable.

Weeks 9–12: Keep controlled technique; change only one thing at a time.

Days 85–90: Finish calmly. No extra catch-up workouts.

FUEL YOUR DAY

Simple Kenyan meal guide

Eat enough to support growth, school, recovery and exercise. This is a flexible example, not a strict menu. Swap foods to fit your family, budget, availability and appetite.

No crash diets, skipped meals, calorie targets, fat burners or bodybuilding supplements. Eat regular meals and snacks when hungry.

The simple plate guide

As a flexible starting point, include vegetables and fruit, protein foods, and starches. Adjust to your hunger and activity.

Water: Drink regularly through the day, and keep water nearby during activity.

Sleep: Teenagers generally need 8–10 hours. Recovery is part of training.
